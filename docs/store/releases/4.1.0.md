## 4.1.0 — Large files keep their real name, and Nexus's download window in the extension's look

This release fixes large files saved as `.txt` and brings Nexus's download window into the extension's look.

- **Large files keep their real name:** Nexus's resumable download, for files over 500 MB in Chrome, Edge and Opera, saved newer files under a code with `.txt`, such as `6f96399b-8cd8-4a91-8fe4-3cdc58d90124.txt`. They had to be renamed by hand before Vortex, Mod Organizer 2 or an archive tool would open them. The save window now offers the file's real name with the right extension (`.zip`, `.7z` or `.rar`), in the form *Import* recognizes. If your browser asks for a click before renaming a finished file, the extension's panel shows a *Fix the file name* button.
- **Nexus's download window in the extension's look:** the *Download mod file* window, with the file's requirements, that the *Manual* and *Vortex* buttons open now has the extension's look. With *Hide ads and Premium panels* on (the default), its "Go premium" banner is hidden. The requirements and the author's note stay.
- **The sign-in panel in the extension's look:** Nexus's "log in or register to download" panel now matches the extension, and its buttons wrap on narrow screens.

Permissions are unchanged, and reporting stays off unless you turn it on.
