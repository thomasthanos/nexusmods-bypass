## 3.1.8 — The requirements, in view

A mod's requirements can show without a click, collection downloads ride out a dropped connection, and **Report on GitHub** now takes a bug report, a feature request or a question. Both browser ZIPs are under **Assets**.

- **Requirements in view:** a new setting, *Open the Requirements section* (off by default, under **Appearance & language**), opens the Requirements section that Nexus shows collapsed on a mod's Description tab. You can still close it, and it stays closed. Asked for in [#29](https://github.com/thomasthanos/nexusmods-bypass/issues/29).
- **Dropped downloads retried:** in Chrome, a collection download cut off by a lost connection could end as "NETWORK_FAILED … cleanup_failed" instead of being tried again. It is retried now, without ever running a second copy beside the first, and **Stop** works on such a download again.
- **Vortex links in collection runs:** the collection panel loads each file page the way a click does, so Nexus answers with the page that carries the `nxm://` link. 3.1.7 did this for single files.
- **Report on GitHub:** the button in **Help & Bugs** opens a menu: *Bug report* (with your diagnostic report, as before), *Feature request* or *Question*, each with its own form and label.
- **Fixes:** the "files downloaded" review ask no longer squeezes its text to one letter per line in a narrow collection panel or in the popup. A **Quick setup** preset is saved in one step, so it is never left half applied.

<summary><strong>Repository & reports</strong> — what changed behind the scenes</summary>

- New issue forms reach the Discord channel once per issue, named *Bug issue*, *Feature request* or *Question*. A form's labels used to post every issue twice.
- Releases take their name from these notes and are announced on Discord when they are published. 3.1.6 and 3.1.7 were never announced.
- The relay baseline moves to 3.1.8: once the Worker is redeployed, reports from 3.1.7 and older still arrive but no longer ping the maintainer.
- Extension permissions are unchanged. Automatic reports remain off unless you turn them on.
